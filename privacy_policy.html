Privacy Policy

Allah's 99 Names (Asma-ul-Husna) Mobile Application

This Privacy Policy explains how Allah's 99 Names handles information when you use our mobile application. We deeply value your privacy and believe in full transparency. Our app is designed to provide an Islamic learning and spiritual experience without collecting personal data.

1 Information We Collect

We do not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contact lists). No user registration or account login is required to use this application.

The application saves the following non-personal data strictly locally on your device:

  • Favorite Names: The names you bookmark for quick access.
  • Practice & Memorisation Progress: Your quiz scores, review history, and learning progress.
  • Puzzle Levels: Completed levels, scores, and unlocked puzzle challenges.
  • Digital Tasbih Counters: Your dhikr counts, target goals, and tasbih history.
  • Audio & Language Preferences: Selected audio language (English / Amharic) and playback preferences.
  • Notification Settings: Scheduled daily reminder preferences.

All of this data is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences) and never leaves your device.

2 How We Use Information

The locally stored information is used solely to:

  • Retain your bookmarked names and favorites
  • Track your memorisation milestones and practice test progress
  • Save unlocked puzzle game levels and achievements
  • Keep track of your digital Tasbih counters across sessions
  • Remember your audio language preference (English / Amharic)
  • Trigger scheduled daily dhikr reminders locally on your device

3 Device Permissions

To deliver core features, the app may request the following device permissions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to deliver local daily reminders to recite and learn the 99 Names of Allah. No remote push notifications or advertisements are sent.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback when you tap the digital Tasbih counter.

You can revoke or enable these permissions at any time through your device's system settings.

4 Audio and Media

All audio recitations and pronunciations (including English and Amharic audio files) are embedded within the application package and played locally on your device.

The app does not track, upload, or share your listening history, audio playback sessions, or device media with any third parties or external servers.

5 Third-Party Services

The application does not integrate third-party tracking, profiling, or behavioral advertising SDKs.

Standard platform-level services provided by Google Play (such as automated crash reports or anonymized installation statistics) may collect non-identifiable technical metrics according to Google's Privacy Policy.

6 Data Security

Because all your data is stored locally within your device's sandboxed storage, your data remains under your direct control. Uninstalling the application will remove all locally stored progress, favorites, and settings from the device.

7 Children's Privacy

Allah's 99 Names is an educational and religious app suitable for general audiences of all ages, including families and children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

8 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future updates, new features, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted here and updated on the Google Play Store listing.

9 Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the app, please contact us via the developer email address provided on the Google Play Store listing.

Privacy First: Allah's 99 Names is designed with complete privacy in mind. Your memorisation progress, Tasbih counts, favorite names, and settings remain 100% on your device.
